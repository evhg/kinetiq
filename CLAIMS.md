## Sealed core (owner, 2026-10-22)

in Switzerland, injected directly into the mould, and lasts three times longer. Told once per place (`copy.ts`): the
reveal in the Unibody chapter (`unibody.sealed`, over the K03 cut-through), the drilled hole that opens a core
(`holes.body`), and the result in Durability (point 1 with its † fine print, `durability.coreFine`).
